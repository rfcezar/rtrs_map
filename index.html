About PoliSoy

PoliSoy (Political Accountability of Soy Business Owners) tracks the political engagement of key asset owners in Brazil’s soy industry, with particular attention to actors certified under sustainability schemes such as RTRS (Roundtable on Responsible Soy). The platform links public data on RTRS audits, corporate ownership records, and campaign donation disclosures from Brazil’s 2022 elections. Contact Rodrigo Cezar for inquiries.
